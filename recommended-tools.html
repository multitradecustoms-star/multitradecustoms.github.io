Pressure washing

Match the machine to the job. Light cleaning and driveways need different power.

  • Electric (about 1,500–2,000 PSI) suits patios, cars, and furniture. Gas (about 2,800–3,200 PSI) suits driveways and heavy dirt.
  • Check GPM too. Cleaning power is PSI times GPM.
  • A nozzle set with a soap nozzle, plus a surface cleaner for flat concrete, saves time.

Gutters and ladders

Clean from a safe position and use the right ladder rating.

  • A telescoping wand or gutter scoop keeps you on the ground longer.
  • Look at the ladder’s duty rating, and pick one rated above your weight plus tools.
  • A ladder stabilizer helps keep the ladder off the gutter.

Landscaping and topiary

Sharp, clean cuts keep plants healthy and shapes crisp.

  • Bypass pruners and a pruning saw for branches
  • Hedge shears or curved topiary shears for precise shaping
  • Shaping frames (spheres, spirals) help hold a form while plants fill in

Snow and ice

Pick tools for the snow you actually get.

  • A wide pusher for light snow and a sturdy ergonomic shovel for heavy loads
  • Rock salt loses strength in very cold weather. Calcium chloride works in colder temperatures.
  • Single-stage snow blowers suit light snow on paved surfaces. Two-stage machines handle heavy snow.

Home tech installs

Mount it safely and keep wiring neat.

  • A stud finder and a laser level for straight, secure mounts
  • A mount rated above your TV’s weight and matching its VESA pattern
  • A cable fish tool for running wire through walls

Safety gear

Protective gear is the cheapest part of any job.

  • Safety glasses and work gloves for every project
  • Hearing protection when running gas tools
  • Non-slip boots and a basic first aid kit

Budget-friendly tools

Smart spending: save on occasional-use items, invest in what you use daily.

  • Budget retailers suit occasional-use hand tools, clamps, drop cloths, hoses, and storage.
  • Spend more on tools you use every week, since they take the most wear.
  • Never cut corners on ladders, safety glasses, hearing protection, or other safety gear.
  • Used tools can be a bargain on marketplaces like eBay. Check seller ratings, photos, and return terms, and avoid used safety gear.
  • Check ratings, warranty terms, and return policies before you buy.
