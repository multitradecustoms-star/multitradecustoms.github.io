How it works

Sourcing: we find the right tool and where to buy it. You buy direct, and we may earn a small sourcing fee.

Special order: you approve a written quote and pay a deposit, we order it from the supplier, and it ships to you.

Consign: you list used equipment with us, we sell it, and you keep the sale price minus our commission, set in a signed agreement.
